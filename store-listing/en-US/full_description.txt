Three Column Method is a small, private, offline notebook for practicing David Burns' three-column technique from "Feeling Good: The New Mood Therapy" — write down an automatic thought, name the cognitive distortion(s) in it, and answer it with a rational response.

THOUGHT RECORDS
• Three-column entries: automatic thought → distortion → rational response
• Before/after belief-strength sliders (0–100%) so you can see the shift
• Burns' cognitive distortions as tappable chips, each with a short description — "Jumping to Conclusions" is split into Mind Reading and Fortune Telling so they can be chosen separately
• An optional situation note for context
• A read-only detail view first, with a share icon to send a formatted copy of any record to another app

DESIGNED FOR YOUR SCREEN
On a tablet, or a phone in landscape, the three columns sit side by side. On a narrower phone the same three sections become their own pages — swipe between them, or tap a title to jump straight there.

FIND WHAT YOU WROTE
A search field filters your thought records and journal entries live as you type. The thought record list is grouped by recency — Today, Yesterday, This Week, This Month, and older by month — so recent entries are always easy to find.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything stays on your device — nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to export or share it yourself. Turn on an optional app lock (fingerprint, face, or your device PIN/pattern) from Settings, and the app will hide its content from the recent-apps screen and block screenshots while locked.

A JOURNAL, TOO
A single-topic notebook for one recurring question: "Why is living in fear of opposition and criticism irrational and unnecessary?" Add a new dated page whenever a fresh thought about it occurs to you, and reorder pages by dragging.

QUIET AND UNDERSTATED
A cool, low-key look — nothing decorative that draws the eye or hints at the screen's contents from across the room — runs through every screen, light or dark, and in English or 简体中文, with an in-app switch for either that's independent of your device settings.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE
Export everything to a single file to back it up, or import a file to restore your data or add many records at once — the app shows you the exact format before you pick a file.
